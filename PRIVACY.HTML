Privacy Policy

Kashmir Suraksha App - Your Safety, Our Priority

Last Updated: April 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Kashmir Suraksha App. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

Important: This app is designed for emergency situations only.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information: Name, Phone Number, Age, Location, District

Automatically Collected: GPS Location, Device Info, Emergency Request History

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Emergency response and service dispatch
  • Location detection for nearest services
  • Service improvement and analysis
  • Security and legal compliance

4. How We Share Your Information

We share information only with relevant government departments: Police, Medical, Fire, and Disaster Management authorities.

Note: We NEVER sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Location Information

Location access is critical for emergency response. You can enable/disable location services through your device settings.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage. No external servers are used.

7. Admin Access

Only authorized government personnel can access emergency requests. Each admin has unique credentials and login tracking.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Check this page for updates.
